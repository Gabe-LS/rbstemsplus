RB Stems Plus 1.4.1: Stems Cache works with rekordbox 7.2.20.

## What's new

- **rekordbox 7.2.20 is supported.**
- **Nothing else changes.** Demucs v4 and the stems Stems Cache has saved stay as they are.

## Updating

Nothing to install: RB Stems Plus 1.4.0 picks up the new list of supported rekordbox versions
the next time it opens. There is no app update and macOS doesn't ask you anything.

If you updated rekordbox to 7.2.20, rekordbox's installer took Stems Cache out: open RB Stems
Plus and click **Install Stems Cache** (it asks for your password).

## Install

Paste this in Terminal and press Return:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/Gabe-LS/rbstemsplus/releases/latest/download/bootstrap.sh | bash
```

The app opens and walks you through the rest. Installing Stems Cache takes about a minute.

## What you need

- A Mac. Tested on macOS 26 Tahoe on an Apple Silicon Mac. Older versions, from macOS 12, should
  work but are untested. Intel Macs should work too, untested; preparing playlists needs Apple
  silicon.
- rekordbox 7.2.17 to 7.2.20 (tested with 7.2.19), with STEMS working (turned on at least
  once).
- About 1.5 GB of free space, and an administrator account for Stems Cache. Prepared stems take
  about 13 to 16 MB per minute of music, depending on the model (AAC files up to twice that).
