RB Stems Plus 1.2: keep saved stems on an external drive.

## What's new

- **Saved stems on an external drive.** In **Settings…**, **Change…** next to *Stems Cache
  location* chooses a drive connected to your Mac. RB Stems Plus makes a folder named *RB Stems Plus Cache*
  there, and Stems Cache saves stems only in it, with the same size and day limits. Switching
  starts empty: the stems saved on this Mac are deleted and saved again on the drive as you load
  tracks. **Use This Mac** goes back; the drive's stems stay on it.
- **Without the drive,** rekordbox separates tracks as usual and nothing is saved. Connect it,
  also while rekordbox runs, and Stems Cache uses it again within seconds: no restart.
- **A renamed drive keeps its stems:** choose it again in Settings and they're found. Several
  Macs or accounts can share one drive's folder, one at a time.
- **The size limit counts the space stems take on the disk,** including the small hidden files
  macOS adds on exFAT and FAT32 drives.

## Updating

RB Stems Plus offers the update when you open it (**RB Stems Plus › Update RB Stems Plus**).
Once updated, RB Stems Plus asks to update Stems Cache too (**Update**, with your password, or
**Not Now**): until then, **Change…** stays off and stems are saved on this Mac as before. As
with every update, macOS asks you to allow RB Stems Plus again when it updates Stems Cache.

Anyone who can write to the drive you choose can change the saved stems rekordbox plays, for
any track in your library. Use a drive you control. Quit rekordbox and eject the drive before
unplugging it.

## Install

Paste this in Terminal and press Return:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/Gabe-LS/rbstemsplus/releases/latest/download/bootstrap.sh | bash
```

The app opens and walks you through the rest. Installing Stems Cache takes about a minute.

## What you need

- A Mac. Tested on macOS 26 Tahoe on an Apple Silicon Mac.
- rekordbox 7.2.17, 7.2.18 or 7.2.19, with STEMS working (turned on at least once).
- About 1.5 GB of free space, and an administrator account for Stems Cache.
