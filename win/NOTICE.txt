RB Stems Plus for Windows: third-party notices
Copyright (c) 2026 Gabe-LS. The code of RB Stems Plus is under the MIT License
(see LICENSE).

This file lists what the Windows files of RB Stems Plus include in addition to
what NOTICE lists. NOTICE comes with this file, in the same folder; it has the
trademark note and the licence texts of Demucs, libFLAC, SQLCipher, pyrekordbox
and ONNX Runtime, which apply to the Windows files as follows:

- The Stems Cache bridge (rbstemsplus-bridge-win-x64.dll, installed as
  rekordbox's onnxruntime.dll) and the prepare helper (rbsp-prepare.exe)
  include libFLAC 1.5.0, built without Ogg and without threads.
- The prepare helper includes SQLCipher 4.19.0, built with LibTomCrypt
  (below) instead of Apple's CommonCrypto. SQLite itself is in the public
  domain.
- The model file (stemsplus-model.onnx) is the same as on the Mac.
- RB Stems Plus does not ship ONNX Runtime, DirectML or libmpg123. On Windows,
  the bridge and the prepare helper use the onnxruntime.dll that comes with
  rekordbox, copied on the user's own PC, and the prepare helper uses
  rekordbox's own DirectML.dll and libmpg123.dll where rekordbox keeps them.
  No file made by AlphaTheta or Pioneer DJ is distributed with RB Stems Plus.
- The C runtime (ucrtbase.dll), Media Foundation, .NET Framework 4.8.1 and WPF
  are parts of Windows and are not shipped.

Every program and library of RB Stems Plus for Windows that is written in C
(all of them except the window app, a .NET program) was built with llvm-mingw
(LLVM and mingw-w64) and includes the first two parts below. The others are in
the files their entries name.

Apple Lossless (ALAC) decoder (compiled into the prepare helper)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The prepare helper decodes ALAC files with Apple's ALAC decoder.

  https://github.com/macosforge/alac
  Copyright (c) 2011 Apple Inc. All rights reserved.
  Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

  RB Stems Plus uses its files ALACBitUtilities.c/.h, dp_dec.c, dplib.h and
  aglib.h unchanged (commit c38887c5c5e64a4b31108733bd79ca9b2496d987). Its
  ALACDecoder.cpp and the dyn_decomp function of its ag_dec.c are ported to C in
  RB Stems Plus's helper/alac.c, a changed version: the C++ class became C
  functions, the samples come out as 32-bit integers, every packet is checked
  against its end, and leading zeros are counted with one instruction.
